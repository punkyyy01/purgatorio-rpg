# {{NOMBRE}} — {{VERSION_VISIBLE}} ({{CANAL}})
*Project page text (English).* **Adventure & exploration RPG for Minecraft 1.21.1: monumental mountain ranges, flowing rivers, ~300 land biomes, dungeons, bosses, classes and magic — no mandatory chapters.**

## A world worth walking
Huge mountain ranges, rivers with real courses, forests, jungles, deserts, swamps and fantasy biomes; a revamped Nether and End; dungeons, ruins, fortresses, ships, villages and special structures spread out calmly. Creatures tied to their ecosystems and bosses worth the trip. There is no quest map here: **you leave, discover, and come back with stories**.

## RPG without becoming an MMO
- **Classes by gear**: wizards, archers, paladins & priests, rogues & warriors (RPG Series). Craft or find your gear; look up any recipe in EMI (press **R**).
- **Animated combat** (Better Combat), **combat roll**, **spells** (Spell Engine).
- **Bosses & beasts**: Cataclysm, Mowzie's Mobs, Bosses of Mass Destruction, Ice and Fire, Born in Chaos, Alex's Caves — the toughest ones live far from spawn. ~2 % of hostile mobs are **elites**.
- **Moderate loot**: dungeon chests give useful things, not end-game gear. Per-player loot chests (Lootr).
- **No grind, no fetch quests.** An **optional** welcome guide; nothing mandatory.

## Bases & multiplayer
No raids or patrols, hostile mobs **do not break blocks**, land claims (Open Parties and Claims), Waystones only in villages and craftable. Building (Create, Supplementaries, Handcrafted…), cooking, storage, fishing and farming.

## Performance & stability
Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, ImmediatelyFast, plus **PURG4TORY Cauces** (own mod, MIT): pre-plans river basins, defers teleports to ungenerated terrain and bounds the server shutdown.

## Requirements
Minecraft **1.21.1** · NeoForge **21.1.259** · Java **21** · **6 GB RAM allocated** (no more than 8) · **a new world is required**.

## ⚠️ This is a Beta
Playable and tested, with real limitations (see the known-issues file). **Make backups.** Do not carry an important world over from an Alpha. For servers use the project's server pack and read `SERVIDOR.md` (4 mods must be downloaded manually because their authors disable third-party distribution).
